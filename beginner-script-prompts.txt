BEGINNER SCRIPT PROMPTS
Copy and paste these into Gemini, Claude or ChatGPT, one at a time, in the SAME chat.
Replace anything in [square brackets] with your own details.

------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT 1 — STUDY THE FORMULA
------------------------------------------------------------
I'm a beginner YouTuber starting a faceless channel in the [your niche] niche.
Below is the script of a video that went viral for a small channel in my niche.

Please study it and break down its formula. For each part of the script, tell me:
1. What section it is (hook, intro, main points, transitions, ending, call to action)
2. What it's doing and why it works (curiosity, a promise, a story, a list, etc.)
3. Roughly how long that section is compared to the whole script

Then summarise the full formula as a simple step-by-step structure I can reuse.
Don't rewrite their script. I only want the structure.

Here is the script:
[paste their script]

------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT 2 — YOUR OWN OUTLINE
------------------------------------------------------------
Now use that exact formula to give me my own outline for a video on:
[your topic / your chosen title]

We're covering the same topic, but do NOT copy their script, their examples or
their wording. Keep the same structure and the same number of sections, and give
me fresh points, examples and angles that are my own.
Show the outline section by section, with one line explaining each point.

------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT 3 — WRITE IT SECTION BY SECTION
------------------------------------------------------------
Let's write the script one section at a time. Start with the hook only.
Follow the formula for this section, keep it natural and easy to read aloud for
a voiceover, and stop when the hook is finished.

(When you're happy with it, send:)
Great. Now write the next section: [section name]. Same rules.

(Repeat until every section is written.)

------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT 4 — CHECK IT
------------------------------------------------------------
Are you following the formula? Compare my script with the original one section
by section and tell me:
1. Where it follows the formula well
2. Anywhere it has copied their words or sentences too closely, and rewrite those
   parts in fresh words
3. Any lines that might sound strange when read by an AI voice, and fix them

------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING LOOKS OFF
------------------------------------------------------------
This doesn't make sense for my topic. Don't copy their words. Use your own
unique words, in a way that makes sense for my topic.

------------------------------------------------------------
REMINDERS
------------------------------------------------------------
- Always read what the AI writes. Sometimes it copies the original script.
- Write section by section, never the whole script at once.
- Keep the competitor's script saved so you can compare it with yours at the end.
